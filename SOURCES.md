# Sources

## Primary texts
- **Mishnah Middot**, chapters 1–5, with Bartenura, Tosfos Yom Tov and Tiferes Yisrael — the measurements of Har HaBayis, the courts, the Mizbeach and the Heichal. English: [Sefaria](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Middot), [Mishnah.org](https://www.mishnah.org/?p=2680), [OU Torah / Mishna Yomit — Rabbi Jack Abramowitz](https://outorah.org/p/21859)
  - 1:3–5 gates of Har HaBayis and the Azarah · 2:1 Har HaBayis 500 × 500 · 2:3 Soreg, Cheil, gates 20 × 10 · 2:4 the low eastern wall · 2:5 Ezras Nashim 135 × 135, four chambers 40 × 40, fifteen semicircular steps · 2:6 Ezras Yisrael & Kohanim 135 × 11, the Duchan · 3:1–4 the Mizbeach · 3:5 rings, tables, pillars · 3:6 the Kiyor, steps of the Ulam · 3:7–8 Ulam entrance, oak beams, Golden Vine · 4:1 Heichal doors · 4:6–7 the Heichal 100 × 100 × 100 · 5:1–2 the Azarah 187 × 135 · 5:3–4 the chambers
- **Mishnah Tamid** (the daily service, Leviim, trumpets, Birkas Kohanim), **Yoma** (Kohen Gadol, Paroches, Even HaShesiyah, Nikanor, Ben Katin), **Shekalim 6:5** (13 shofar chests), **Sukkah 51a–b** (Simchas Beis HaSho'eivah, the gallery), **Zevachim 53–54, 62, 88b** (altar, garments), **Menachos 28b, 98b–99b** (Menorah, tables), **Arachin 2:6**, **Keilim 1:8–9** (levels of holiness), **Avos 5:5**.
- **Shemos 25–30, 39** — the keilim and the bigdei kehunah.
- **Rambam**, *Hilchos Beis HaBechirah* and his diagrams in the Peirush HaMishnayos.
- **Josephus**, *Antiquities* 15.11 and *Wars* 5.5 — the stoas, the white marble, the gold.

## Clothing
- Kohanim and Kohen Gadol: Shemos 28; Yoma 7:5; Zevachim 88b — [Ohr Somayach, Taamei HaMitzvos](https://ohr.edu/12430), [Ptil Tekhelet — the Me'il](https://www.tekhelet.com/ec-meil/)
- Ordinary dress of the period (tunic, tallis with tzitzis and a thread of techeiles, head coverings, sandals) follows the general descriptions in the Mishnah (Shabbos 6, Keilim) and archaeological finds as shown in the Avi-Yonah model.
